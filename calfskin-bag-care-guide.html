Caring for a Calfskin Bag

Calfskin rewards a little care with decades of good looks. These simple habits keep the leather supple, the colour rich and the shape crisp.

I

Everyday habits

Wipe your bag with a soft, dry cloth every week or so to remove dust. Keep hand cream, pens and newly dyed denim away from light-coloured leather. Empty heavy items when you can, because constant weight stretches handles and straps.

II

Cleaning gently

Always test any product on a hidden area first.

  • Use a slightly damp cloth for most marks.
  • For stubborn spots, use a cleaner made for smooth leather.
  • Never soak leather or use household cleaners.
III

Conditioning

Leather slowly loses its natural oils. Apply a thin layer of leather conditioner two or three times a year, or when the leather feels dry. Less is more: too much can darken the colour or leave the surface sticky.

IV

Rain and scratches

If your bag gets wet, blot it with a soft towel and let it dry naturally, away from radiators and direct sun. Light scratches on smooth calfskin can often be softened by gently rubbing them with a clean fingertip; deeper marks are best left to a leather specialist.

V

Storing well

Stuff the bag with acid-free tissue to keep its shape, fasten the closures and store it upright in a breathable dust bag. Avoid plastic, which traps moisture. Keep it out of sunlight, which fades colour, and give it room so the leather is not creased.
