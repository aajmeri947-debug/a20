Calfskin, Lambskin & Cowhide

Most fine handbags are made from one of three leathers. Each has its own character, and knowing the difference helps you choose a bag you will love for years.

I

Calfskin

Leather from young cattle. The hide is thinner and the grain finer than adult cowhide, so calfskin feels smooth and supple yet is still strong. It takes dye beautifully and can be finished smooth, pebbled or brushed, which is why it is the leading choice for fine bags.

II

Lambskin

The softest of the three, prized for its buttery feel.

  • Very light and drapes softly.
  • Marks and scratches easily.
  • Best for evening bags and careful owners.
III

Cowhide

Leather from adult cattle. It is thicker, heavier and very durable, with a more visible grain. Cowhide suits hard-wearing totes, briefcases and travel bags that need to carry weight and take knocks.

IV

How the finish changes things

A smooth, polished calfskin shows every scratch but looks elegant. A pebbled or embossed calfskin hides marks and resists rain far better. Brushed calfskin and nubuck feel velvety but need gentle care. The finish matters as much as the animal.

V

Choosing for your life

For a bag you will use every day, a pebbled calfskin is hard to beat. For a structured work bag, choose smooth box calf. Save lambskin for special occasions, and choose cowhide when strength matters more than softness.
